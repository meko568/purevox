Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

Is PureVox really free?

Yes. PureVox is free and open source — no subscription, no watermark, no feature paywall. If it saves you time, you can support development with a donation.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Separation runs entirely on your own machine with AI models stored locally. Your footage never leaves your disk — there is no server and no account.

Which platforms does PureVox support?

Windows and macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) installers, plus Linux AppImage, .deb and .rpm packages. No mobile app yet — on phones and tablets you can run PureVox for free in Google Colab instead.

How does it remove background music?

PureVox uses Demucs, an AI model that splits the audio track into stems — voice, music and effects — then rebuilds the video with only the voice stem. Dialogue stays untouched while the music disappears.

Will it run on an old or low-spec computer?

Probably. PureVox picks models and processes files in chunks automatically to keep memory use in check. If your machine is very limited, the CLI tool is lighter, or you can use the Colab notebook which runs on Google's hardware.

What happens if my laptop dies mid-job?

Nothing is lost. Jobs are resumable — reopen PureVox and pick the job back up exactly where it left off.
